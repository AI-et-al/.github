Needle & Wave

Learn why a needle, a wave, and a Fourier series are the same problem, in about 25 minutes.

What you'll learn
  • How any periodic signal is built from sines, and what an Lp norm measures.
  • Why narrow in time forces wide in frequency, and why that puts tubes everywhere.
  • How Besicovitch turned a needle in almost no area, and what a set of dimension 2 with zero area is.
  • Why tubes in different directions refuse to overlap: the Kakeya maximal conjecture.
  • Why waves can focus for an instant but not for an interval: local smoothing, the top of the chain.
