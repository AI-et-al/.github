The Exponential Wall

Learn what P vs NP, SAT, and the Exponential Time Hypothesis really say by solving puzzles, in about 25 minutes.

What you'll learn

  • What a 3-SAT formula is, by satisfying a dozen of them by hand.
  • Why 2^n is a wall that faster hardware cannot climb.
  • How real solvers prune the search tree, and why the worst case stays exponential.
  • The difference between polynomial, subexponential, and 2^(n^ε) running times.
  • What falls, and what does not, if the Exponential Time Hypothesis is false.

Seven short levels. Progress is saved in this browser. Keyboard works everywhere; digits toggle switches.
